The map

Select any row to see what each regime requires, with sources. Rows tagged with an industry came from that overlay and were then checked against every other regime.

How this was built

The baseline is what a clinical or biological lab lives under in California: federal law, state law, and Alameda County / San Francisco. Seven other regimes were then mapped row by row against the same requirements: nuclear (NRC), commercial aviation (FAA and NTSB), banking (OCC, Fed, FDIC, FinCEN), pharma (FDA), chemical process safety (OSHA, EPA, CSB), the bulk power grid (FERC and NERC), and the drug supply chain with its cross-sector levers.

Each overlay added the requirements its own field has and labs don't. Every regime was then scored on every one of those added rows, so a blank cell is a finding: someone looked and found no equivalent.

Cells cite statutes and regulations directly where the source could be reached (U.S. Code, eCFR, Cornell LII, govinfo, agency sites). A cell that leans on a law firm, news report or trade summary carries a secondary source tag.

Every cell was checked by a separate reviewer against its sources, and every status call was checked again. Status is strict and applied the same way to every column: proposed rules, guidance, funding conditions, asset thresholds that leave most of the field out, and voluntary commitments are not counted as binding law. The AI column covers frontier developers in California and San Francisco, federal law included.

The requirement list starts from labs on purpose. The claim being tested is that we already know how to run hazardous work responsibly, so the yardstick is the regime built for it. Where a row was written for physical hazards, the AI cell says what the equivalent would be (moving model weights, a release outside the sandbox) and is scored on that.

Voluntary practice at AI companies is real and some of it is serious work. It is shown as voluntary, not counted as law, because it can be changed or dropped by the company that wrote it.

Disclosure: this map was researched and checked with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. Anthropic is one of the companies in the AI column and a signatory to the Sept 29, 2026 White House Accord. The framing is mine. The conclusions are yours to draw: every cell links to its source so you can check it without trusting the tool or me.
